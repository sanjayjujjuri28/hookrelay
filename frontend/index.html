Every outbound event is signed with a unique per-endpoint webhook secret. Receivers verify signatures to guarantee origin and prevent payload tampering.

Never drop critical business events. When destination servers return 4xx/5xx or experience network timeouts, HookRelay retries automatically with progressive backoff.
